B2: Masking AMOUNTs in states that report them well
Pre-registration, written 2026-10-30 20:25 PT, before any B2 result was computed.

Disclosure. An exploratory version was run earlier tonight (mask_amounts_real.R): on all 820 clean_split
cases, masking AMOUNTs gave 47.3% agreement with the AMOUNT-guided result (82% where slot 1 carries >= 90%).
The thresholds below were set knowing that figure. They were not tuned to it, and no B2 numbers had been seen.

1. Data and code
- Input: the pre-Step-7 snapshot (scratch/pre7_env.RData, built from the tracked script at 2570391).
- Code: that snapshot's copies of recreate_elements() and amount_pattern(). No tracked file is used or edited.

2. Which state-years "report well" (fixed in advance)
- Unit: state x fiscal year.
- A state-year reports well when both hold:
  (a) at least 10 multi-element cases are classified clean_split;
  (b) informative cases are at least 20% of its multi-element cases. Informative means clean_split, plus
      "other" cases whose positive AMOUNTs sum to within $2 of AMTERR and where every present slot has
      AMOUNT > 0. The second kind is good reporting with missing or mixed E_FINDG signs.
- The "other" cases count toward the share only. They are not evaluation cases, because the AMOUNT-guided
  (capped) path, which supplies the truth, does not run on them.
- Sensitivity run: all clean_split cases nationally, whatever their state-year.

3. Evaluation cases
- clean_split cases in report-well state-years that the unmasked run puts on the capped path
  (cascade_type == "capped") and that reach RAWBEN exactly.
- Truth: the raw values from that unmasked, AMOUNT-guided run.

4. Masking arms (they mirror the real reporting patterns)
- zero: AMOUNT1-9 = 0; E_FINDG kept as reported.
- copied: every present slot = AMTERR; every E_FINDG = the case direction (2 if RAWBEN > FSBEN, else 3).
- amount1_only: AMOUNT1 = AMTERR, other slots 0; E_FINDG kept.
- After masking, amount_pattern() is recomputed, as the code would do.

5. Fallbacks compared on the masked cases
- F0 (current code): recreate_elements() on the masked case. This is the greedy cascade in slot order,
  utilities first.
- F1 (proportional split): the gap RAWBEN - FSBEN is split across slots in proportion to empirical slot
  shares, and each element then moves the benefit by its share, as on the capped path. Mechanics:
  AMOUNTi = share_i x AMTERR, every E_FINDG = the case direction, amount_pattern forced to "clean_split".
  Shares are the mean |AMOUNTi| / sum|AMOUNT| by slot, within the element mix (the variable class of each
  slot, in slot order, e.g. "earn|rent"). A mix with fewer than 10 training cases uses the training mean
  share by slot position for that number of elements.
- F2 (typical-largest-first order): the greedy cascade with slots ordered by each variable class's mean
  AMOUNT share in training, not by slot. Mechanics: AMOUNTi = that class mean, amount_pattern forced to
  "other", so the code orders by AMOUNT and moves greedily.
- F1 and F2 depend only on the element codes, so they give the same result under every masking arm. They
  are run once, on the zero arm.
- Out of sample: report-well state-years are split at random (seed 20261030) into two halves. Shares are
  fitted on one half and applied to the other, and then the reverse (two-fold cross-fitting by state-year
  cluster). Every evaluated case is therefore scored with shares that did not use its own state-year.

6. Metrics, per case and against the truth
- all_agree: every Step 7 variable is within max($10, 2% of the truth value) of the truth. Primary.
- var_agree: the same test per variable, for variables the truth or the arm moved.
- MAE: sum over variables of |arm - truth|, in dollars.
- reached: the recreated benefit equals RAWBEN exactly.
- slot1_share: the share of the case's gap (RAWBEN minus the starting benefit) produced by moving only
  slot 1's variable from its start to its reconstructed value. The attribution error is
  |slot1_share(arm) - slot1_share(truth)|; attr_agree means that error is at most 0.10.
- Intervals: cluster bootstrap by state-year, 1,000 resamples, for the headline rates and for the
  differences F1 - F0 and F2 - F0.

7. Breakdowns
- Number of elements (2, 3+).
- Element mix (mixes with at least 15 cases).
- State.
- Household size (1, 2, 3, 4+).
- Truth slot-1 share (>= 0.9 vs < 0.9). This breakdown was added because of the exploratory result.

8. Grading the greedy fallback (F0, zero arm, all_agree)
- Good: at least 75%.
- Acceptable: 50% to under 75%.
- Poor: under 50%.
- A fallback beats F0 if its all_agree is at least 5 points higher, the 95% cluster-bootstrap interval of
  the difference excludes 0, and its mean MAE is not higher.
- Equal within 5 points means no preference. The simpler rule, F0, then stays.

9. Limitation stated in advance
- Report-well states (a handful, e.g. WV, MT, VA, ND, IL) may differ from all_zero states in their error
  mix and in how they split errors.
- The capped-path truth assumes QC's AMOUNTs are correct, and that the code's order (utilities, then
  largest AMOUNT) matches the order QC used to compute them.
